MASTER SERVICES AND SUPPLIER AGREEMENT

This Master Services and Supplier Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026, by and between ABC Enterprise Solutions LLC ("Buyer") and Global Tech Logistics Inc. ("Supplier").

--- PAGE 1 ---
SECTION 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES AND ORDER QUANTITIES
1.1 Scope. Supplier agrees to manufacture, supply, and deliver specialized technical components in accordance with purchase orders issued by Buyer.
1.2 Order Quantities & Bulk Discount. Standard pricing applies to orders under 1,000 units. If order quantity exceeds 1,000 units, a 5% volume discount applies to the total contract order value.

--- PAGE 2 ---
SECTION 2. DELIVERY SCHEDULE AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
2.1 Delivery Timeline. Supplier shall complete delivery of all scheduled goods within 10 days of purchase order confirmation ("Delivery Deadline").
2.2 Delivery Delay Penalty. If delivery is delayed beyond 10 days, a liquidated damages penalty of 2% of the contract value applies for each applicable week of delay, subject to a maximum penalty cap of 10% of contract value.

--- PAGE 3 ---
SECTION 3. PAYMENT TERMS AND OVERDUE INTEREST
3.1 Invoice & Payment Deadline. Buyer shall issue payment within 30 days of invoice receipt.
3.2 Late Payment Interest. If payment is delayed beyond 30 days, interest of 1.5% per month will be charged on the outstanding invoice balance until fully settled.

--- PAGE 4 ---
SECTION 4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & PERFORMANCE
4.1 System Uptime Requirement. Supplier warrants that the supply telemetry platform shall maintain an operational uptime of at least 99.5% each calendar month.
4.2 SLA Violation Deduction. If monthly uptime drops below 99.5%, a penalty deduction of $500 per 0.1% uptime incident applies, up to a maximum penalty limit of $5,000 per month.

--- PAGE 5 ---
SECTION 5. TERM AND RENEWAL
5.1 Term. This Agreement shall remain in effect for an initial period of one (1) year.
5.2 Auto-Renewal. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days prior to the expiration of the current term.
